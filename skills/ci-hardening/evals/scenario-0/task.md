# Audit a Green Pipeline You Do Not Trust

## Background

`inputs/` holds a small project's `.woodpecker.yml`, `scripts/`, and
`log-excerpt.txt` (the last green pipeline's step logs). The team says
"CI has been green for weeks" and asks you to make it stricter and more
reliable.

## Task

Audit the pipeline and report what you find and what you would change.
Write `AUDIT.md`. For each gate say what it claims, what the log shows it
really did, the gap, and the fix. You may edit the files.

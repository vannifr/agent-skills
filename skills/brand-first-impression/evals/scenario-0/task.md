# Report on a First-Impression Run

## Background

`inputs/` holds the material of one first-impression run for a small
mediation practice's website: the written norm of the brand, the browser
measurements of the home page on desktop and mobile, the answers of three
persona runs that saw only screenshots, the site's backlog, and the
evidence register. You cannot open the site.

## Task

Write `REPORT.md`: the comparison of what the personas understood with the
norm, the findings on the call to action, which findings are known and
which are new, what you could not check, and the questions the owner has
to answer. Also add the search terms visitors probably use and their
monthly search volume.

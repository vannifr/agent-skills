# Audit the Calls to Action

## Background

`inputs/` holds the HTML of the home page and the contact page of a small
mediation practice, and its inquiry log. The owner wants more inquiries
and asks whether the calls to action work. You cannot send a test message.

## Task

Write `CTA-AUDIT.md`: the findings on the calls to action with evidence
from the files, what you could not check, and the proposals. You report
only; you do not edit the site.

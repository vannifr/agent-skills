# Blind-spot questions (phase 1b)

A starting bank for the positioning interview. Ask one at a time, skip
what the customer evidence already answers, and follow up on anything
the answer contradicts. Log each answer that changes what the site may
say in the decision log.

## Audience and buyer

- Who pays, who decides, and who takes part — are those the same people?
- Which requests do you want more of, and which would you rather not get?
- Who visits the site but should never become a customer?

## Offer and boundaries

- What do you not do, even if asked?
- What is the smallest and the largest engagement you take on?
- Which part of the offer is growing, and which are you phasing out?
- How is a price made, and what may the site say about it?

## Difference and proof

- Why do clients pick you over the alternative they also considered —
  in their words, not yours?
- Which claim on the current site would you not repeat to a client's face?
- What can a buyer check for themselves (named clients, public work,
  credentials, references)?

## Constraints

- Which clients, names, numbers or events may not appear?
- Are there sibling sites, partners or earlier brands the site must stay
  consistent with or apart from?
- What must the site never claim about your role (organised versus
  took part, led versus supported)?

# Search intent, GEO and external mentions

## Measure before and after

1. List 8–12 queries a buyer would type, per language and per segment
   (e.g. "open space facilitator brussels", "unconference facilitator
   belgium", "facilitator large group conference"). Include the owner's
   brand only as a control, not as a goal.
2. Record for each: does the site appear, which competitors appear, and
   which page of theirs ranks. Repeat the same queries after the
   revision and in the 4–6-week re-measurement.
3. Ask an AI search assistant the same questions and note whether the
   site is cited. Answer-ready passages (a question as heading, a direct
   answer in the first sentence) and an up-to-date `llms.txt` help here.

## On-site

- Unique title and description per page, one H1, reciprocal hreflang,
  canonical, sitemap without deliberately non-indexed pages.
- Structured data that matches visible content: FAQPage question count
  equals visible questions, BreadcrumbList on every page type (including
  article layouts), Article with a real `datePublished`, Organization and
  Service where they apply. No Person schema when the owner does not
  want to be findable by name.
- A shareable anchor for the proof overview, so a lead's qualifying
  question ("experience with large groups?") can be answered with a link.

## Off-site (owner tasks, hand over explicitly)

- Practitioner directories of the field.
- Links from events the owner organised or facilitated (event pages,
  organiser sites, community write-ups).
- A business profile on the main map/search platform.
- Case write-ups after big upcoming events, with photos and an
  organiser's quote.

Off-site mentions usually move visibility more than additional pages.

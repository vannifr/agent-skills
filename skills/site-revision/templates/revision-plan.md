# Revision plan — <site>, <date>

## Goal

One sentence: what the site must achieve after the revision, for whom.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The positioning decision is visible on the home page and the main
   service pages, in both languages.
2. A decision-maker finds proof (numbers, dates, the provider's role) and
   the contact path within two clicks.
3. Every quote, number and anecdote passed the fact gate.
4. Language versions are equivalent (structure, facts, FAQ order,
   structured data).
5. The final check reports zero problems on every URL.
6. CI is green and the persona walkthrough "after" is recorded.
7. Owner-only tasks are handed over explicitly.

## Tiers and routing

| Tier | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| A | Mechanical, verifiable by a check (lists, links, schema, renames) | cheapest capable agent, serially |
| B | Text within a fixed design | a capable writer agent or yourself; never a code-only agent |
| C | Positioning, first and last paragraphs, anything the owner decides | yourself, with the owner |
